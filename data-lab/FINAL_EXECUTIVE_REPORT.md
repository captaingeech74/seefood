# SeeFood Data Grand Experiment — Final Executive Report

## Decision

**No-Go / Redirect. Do not run Cycle 10.**

The DataLab bore fruit as a measurement and falsification program. It did not
produce a game-changing data-acquisition path: verified improvement is zero
comparison dishes, zero national coverage points, and zero valid Customer
uploads.

## What we learned

- SeeFood's six production comparison claims were stored-signal claims, not
  verified coverage. Full review verified 0 of 21 claimed comparison dishes.
- DoorDash item photos can align extremely well in selected packets: 168/168
  exact in the strongest bounded sample. That sample was Temecula-selected,
  had unreviewed rights, and supplied no national source-attempt denominator.
- The in-app contribution machinery became more rigorous, but never became
  pilot-ready and produced no real contribution or conversion evidence.
- Tattle is a credible partnership idea, not acquired evidence. Exact
  photo-to-order-line binding, export, rights, deletion, yield, and economics
  remain unproved without a controller packet.

## What SeeFood should build now

Build the core product around honest, rights-cleared first-party data:
merchant-authorized menu/photo capture, clear empty states, and the corrected
measurement contract. Preserve the useful inactive contribution infrastructure
but do not enable or market it as a proven growth loop.

## Negotiate or monitor

Keep the exact DoorDash bespoke-data and Tattle controller requests as business
development briefs. Monitor merchant-authorized paths such as Google Business
Profile, Flipdish, Square, and GloriaFood. Reopen evidence work only when a
real authorization, schema/rights packet, or merchant grant arrives.

## Stop doing

Stop treating DataLab as an active game-changing acquisition program. Do not
spend another cycle on stored replay, parser/schema capability, DL-007 repair,
or projections from raw rows, platform footprint, selected precision, or
unreviewed public photos. Do not scrape or conduct outreach without separate
authorization.

## Confidence and cost

Confidence in **No-Go / Redirect under the fixed finish line is high**.
Confidence in national yield, usable rights, and Customer conversion for the
preserved opportunities is absent because none was measured. Monetary
experiment cost was **$0**; DataLab made no production change or live
treatment.

## Concrete next action for Kyle

Close and archive the DataLab portfolio now. Move SeeFood to the ordinary
product roadmap and retain the DoorDash/Tattle briefs for a human business
decision. Kyle need take no immediate action; if a controller later supplies
the requested evidence or permission, evaluate it as a separately authorized
new program—not Cycle 10.
