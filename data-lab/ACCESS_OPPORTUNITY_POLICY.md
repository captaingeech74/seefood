# Access Opportunity Policy

## Mission

The DataLab searches for the highest-leverage ways to create strongly matched
Management-versus-Customer comparison dishes. It must search beyond sources
that SeeFood can use immediately. Partner APIs, licensed datasets, restricted
exports, merchant-mediated access, custom exceptions, and bespoke data deals
are valid discoveries when they could materially change coverage.

The value case is national. Temecula is the development slice, not the target
market ceiling. A high-quality menu or Management-photo path may be strategically
transformative on its own, but only when it has credible national reach; the
claim that it will attract Customer contributions must be tested rather than
assumed.

The lab distinguishes discovery from execution:

- Discovery asks whether the data would be valuable, what appears to exist, who
  controls it, and what agreement would make a test possible.
- Execution accesses or collects data only when that access is authorized and
  fits the DataLab safety boundary.

This policy widens the opportunity set. It does not authorize bypassing a
control, violating terms, misusing credentials, or treating inaccessible data
as acquired coverage.

## Three Independent Ratings

Every source or access path receives all three ratings.

### 1. Technical Value

Rate High, Medium, Low, or Unknown using:

- incremental restaurants likely to gain a real comparison dish;
- restaurant and current-menu identity quality;
- direct item-to-image linkage;
- Management or Customer author provenance;
- uniqueness beyond the frozen source stack;
- geographic reach, freshness, refreshability, and expected economics; and
- the strength of measured or cited evidence.

For High technical value, require a credible national scaling path. A
Temecula-only gain may validate mechanics but cannot establish strategic value.

Do not reduce this rating merely because access is currently restricted.
Projected value must be labeled `inferred`; only authorized, independently
measured value may be labeled `verified`.

### 2. Current Access Posture

Use one primary label:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Open/Public | Usable without individualized permission, subject to documented constraints |
| Merchant-authorized | A restaurant can deliberately grant access to its own data |
| Partner-only | The controller requires an approved platform relationship |
| Commercial license required | A standard paid license or subscription appears available |
| Custom permission required | A bespoke exception, pilot, export, or data agreement is needed |
| Terms/rights unclear | Access or downstream usage rights cannot yet be established |
| Observable but unauthorized | The capability is technically visible, but SeeFood lacks permission to test or collect it |
| Prohibited/unsafe | Testing would require evasion, credential misuse, harmful access, or another unacceptable act |
| Unknown | Evidence is insufficient to classify |

Multiple delivery paths for the same source may have different postures and
must be recorded separately.

### 3. Access Action

Use one:

- **Test now:** an authorized, bounded, lab-safe probe is available.
- **Pursue permission:** ask a merchant, platform, data owner, or other
  gatekeeper for a pilot or exception.
- **Pursue a commercial deal:** investigate licensing, pricing, minimums, and
  contract rights.
- **Monitor:** retain the lead and watch for a change in access or product
  posture.
- **Do not pursue:** technical value is weak, evidence is disproven, economics
  are untenable, or the only apparent path is prohibited/unsafe.

`Reject` applies to the technical or evidentiary hypothesis. Lack of current
authorization by itself is not a reason to reject a high-value source.
`Quarantine` means the evidence or output cannot count as benchmark truth yet.

## Allowed Discovery

Using public and already-authorized evidence, the lab may:

- identify public, undocumented, partner-only, private, and restricted
  capabilities at a high level;
- map visible schemas, delivery mechanisms, likely coverage, and controlling
  organizations;
- review official documentation, public client behavior, terms, pricing,
  partner programs, patents, talks, job postings, and credible technical
  reporting;
- make bounded image reads from public or already-authorized recorded locators
  when an active experiment fixes the request/image cap, keeps bytes in ignored
  lab storage, and records failures, runtime, and cost;
- model value from defensible public samples while labeling the estimate; and
- design a permission-gated validation without executing it.

The lab may not:

- bypass authentication, authorization, paywalls, rate controls, bot controls,
  or other technical restrictions;
- obtain, guess, share, or reuse credentials or tokens outside their authorized
  purpose;
- access private endpoints or collect restricted data without permission;
- recommend evasion as an acquisition method; or
- claim inferred or inaccessible supply as current SeeFood coverage.

## Deal Brief

For every High-value source whose action is Pursue permission or Pursue a
commercial deal, record:

1. Controller and likely human decision-maker.
2. Exact data, fields, rights, geography, and refresh access requested.
3. Smallest bounded pilot and success criteria.
4. Why the source is uniquely valuable to comparison coverage.
5. Value SeeFood can offer: attribution, traffic, merchant tools, revenue,
   feedback, co-marketing, or another concrete exchange.
6. Provenance, privacy, retention, attribution, and derivative-use terms that
   must be settled.
7. Delivery, identifiers, update cadence, service levels, and security needs.
8. Pricing structure, minimum commitment, and a stop ceiling to negotiate.
9. Expected coverage upside if the pilot succeeds, labeled as an estimate and
   never as verified until the authorized pilot is measured.
10. Fallback if permission or economics fail.

Kyle or the main SeeFood thread decides whether to contact a source, negotiate,
spend money, or authorize an access path. The DataLab supplies the evidence and
the smallest useful ask.

## Promotion

Two recommendation types are valid:

- **Implementation recommendation:** authorized access has been tested,
  independently evaluated, and passed the normal promotion gates.
- **Opportunity recommendation:** technical potential is evidenced, current
  access is explicit, and a concrete permission or deal path exists. It remains
  unmeasured potential and is never integrated automatically by the lab.
