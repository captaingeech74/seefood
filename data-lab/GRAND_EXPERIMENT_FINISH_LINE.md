# SeeFood Data Grand Experiment — Finish Line

## Current Position

The grand experiment has established three facts:

1. SeeFood's verified comparison baseline is zero, despite six production SQL
   claims.
2. The selected DoorDash-heavy Management packet has excellent item-to-photo
   alignment, but national yield, original provenance, and display rights are
   not proved.
3. First-party Customer contribution is mechanically plausible, but DL-007 is
   closed as not pilot-ready. Cycle 6 fixed database/runtime parity but failed
   the fixed route-retry and every-gate terminal-evidence requirements.

This is promising evidence, not yet a defensible go or no-go conclusion.

complete; the remaining likely path is:

1. **Cycle 6 — complete, failed:** parity passed, but the exact retry and

Cycle 10 is unallocated reserve. Use it only if Cycles 7 or 8 expose one
specific, bounded uncertainty capable of changing the final decision. Do not

## Early Stops

- Cycle 6 failed its exact predeclared parity/state-machine gate. DL-007 is
  closed as not pilot-ready; no further repair cycle or treatment is
  authorized.
- If a Customer-behavior cycle reaches its bound and fails the behavioral

- If a Management-source cycle cannot test national yield without new

- End immediately when the evidence supports a defensible final decision. Do
  not continue merely because budget remains.

## Defensible Finish Line

The grand experiment ends with exactly one of these decisions:

### Go

At least one nationally scalable path clears a game-changer bar:

- **Management path:** independently projected to add at least 20 percentage
  points of current-menu-plus-strong-Management-photo restaurant coverage on a
  locked national frame across at least two strata, with at least 95%
  exact/strong item precision and a 95% Wilson lower bound of at least 90% on
  at least 35 audited records, a repeatable refresh path, measured
  failures/duplicates/cost, and an explicit usable-rights or controller-deal
  path; or
- **Customer path:** a real bounded treatment produces at least 35 valid,
  rights-granted, exact/strong item-linked Customer uploads, at least 1.5%
  valid-upload conversion among analysis-eligible prompt recipients, at least
  95% item precision with a 95% Wilson lower bound of at least 90%, zero
  wrong-restaurant or same-image pairs, and economics that plausibly scale to
  materially more comparison dishes nationally.

### Conditional Go

Technical yield clears the same game-changer bar, but access or rights require
one identified controller decision. A Customer pilot with 10–34 valid uploads
may also qualify only as Conditional Go when point precision is at least 95%
and no wrong-restaurant or same-image pair appears. The result must include the
exact data and rights ask, smallest pilot, expected economics, and a stop
ceiling. Projected records alone do not qualify.

### No-Go / Redirect

No tested path clears either bar within the bounded cycles, or the only strong
path depends on access, cost, or behavior that is not realistically attainable.
Preserve useful product infrastructure, but stop treating the DataLab as a
game-changing data program.

## Reporting Contract

After each cycle:

- the main lead reports one verdict, what changed, exact tests/evidence, cost,
  remaining risk, and its recommendation;
- DataLab independently reports one verdict, verified improvement versus
  baseline, confidence, cost, and whether the finish line moved; and
- no next cycle begins until both reports exist and Kyle's authorization
  permits the required scope.
