# DL-012 — Tattle Controller Schema, Rights, And Yield Gate, Cycle 8

## Preregistered Decision Target

Determine whether preserved public/controller evidence plus any
controller-authorized packet available at the start of Cycle 8 establishes a
real, nationally scalable exact-item Customer-photo path capable of changing
the grand experiment decision.

This bound is frozen before applying the Cycle 8 stop gate.

## Hypothesis

A controller-authorized Tattle packet establishes:

1. immutable photo-to-exact-order-line/item binding, including multi-item
   handling;
2. a supported photo export/API delivery path with deletion state;
3. participating-controller authority and Customer consent for SeeFood
   retention, pairing, display, derived labels, required sublicensing, and
   deletion propagation;
4. aggregate outcomes for at least 1,000 already-sent, lawfully consented
   prompts; and
5. at least 35 blinded historical photo records across at least 10 locations,
   sufficient to measure exact/strong item precision, rights validity,
   usefulness, duplicates, Management-photo overlap, valid-upload conversion,
   unique-location yield, and national economics.

Public product claims, contact channels, projected records, schema guesses, or
an unverified sender identity cannot pass this hypothesis.

## Bound

- Inspect only Tattle evidence already preserved in the DataLab worktree and
  the main thread's verified Cycle 8 handoff facts.
- Do not repeat broad source discovery or make any web, API, email, form,
  browser, phone, or social request.
- DataLab must not contact Tattle.
- Do not use the connected Gmail identity because it is not Kyle or a verified
  SeeFood business identity.
- Do not use demo contact data, invent a SeeFood sender address, impersonate
  Kyle, submit a form, create an account, or ask another party to relay
  outreach.
- Use no credential, production system, customer list, personal data, paid
  service, or external quota.
- Do not count public footprint, survey participation, raw photos, schema
  capability, or modeled uploads as coverage.
- Money ceiling: $0. External request ceiling: 0. Outreach ceiling: 0. New
  records/images ceiling: 0.

Optional descriptors such as ghost-kitchen status and opening recency are not
requirements.

## Stop Gate

Stop Cycle 8 as `Needs a decision / access-blocked` when no
controller-authorized packet exists, no verified SeeFood sender is available,
or preserved public evidence fails to resolve any of:

- exact photo-to-order-line binding;
- supported photo export;
- participating-controller and Customer rights for SeeFood;
- deletion propagation;
- the 1,000-prompt funnel denominator;
- the 35-photo audit packet; or
- national unique-location comparison yield and economics.

On stop:

- verified coverage improvement remains zero;
- technical value remains separate from access posture;
- preserve the exact request/deal path;
- do not contact Tattle or substitute synthetic evidence; and
- select Cycle 9 final portfolio synthesis without starting it.

## Preregistered Decisions

- **Keep:** every documentary and real-yield gate is independently evidenced.
- **Revise / access-blocked:** technical potential remains, but controller
  evidence or authorized access is absent.
- **Reject:** authorized real evidence disproves the technical/yield thesis.
- **Quarantine:** supplied evidence cannot be trusted or safely used.

Access action is recorded separately from evidence decision.
