# DataLab 2.0 Initial Plan

## Cycle 0 — Pick the Backbone Strategy

Compare three nationally scalable operating models using current official
information and real samples: one broad open seed, one commercial seed, and a
minimal complementary-source combination. Evaluate coverage, restaurant
precision, stable IDs, address/coordinates, websites, phone, status, freshness,
access, cost, update mechanics, and incremental value between sources. Choose
the simplest effective model by the end of the cycle. No external source is the
guaranteed answer.

Deliver a source-neutral identity schema and working bounded loaders into a
local shadow database. Preserve all source identities and observations so the
internal entity can survive a supplier change.

## Cycle 1 — National Reality Check

Run the anchor on a practical national sample across regions, large and small
communities, chains, and independents. Audit errors and missing key fields.
Estimate usable national scale without building an elaborate holdout program.

## Cycle 2 — Identity Enrichment

Test the best scalable way to add websites/provider identities and identify
high-confidence omissions, closures, moves, and duplicates. Prefer a small
number of complementary sources over source accumulation.

## Cycle 3 — DoorDash National Yield

Measure store discovery, current menu yield, item-photo yield, linkage quality,
overlap, refresh behavior, blocks, runtime, and likely national scale on a real
sample. Separate technical value from any agreement needed for production.

## Cycle 4 — Restaurant Websites and Ordering Platforms

Measure menus and item photos available through restaurant-owned websites,
structured data, and recurring embedded ordering providers. Identify which
provider-specific connector gives the largest incremental lift.

## Cycle 5 — Best Remaining Bet and Final Ranking

Use the preceding evidence to test only the highest-value unresolved channel or
integration. Then publish the ranked source plan and concrete main-team backlog.
Stop.
