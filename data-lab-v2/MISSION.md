# DataLab 2.0 Mission

Give SeeFood a practical national data-acquisition plan and working evidence for
the best additions. The order of work is:

1. one dependable SeeFood-owned restaurant identity graph fed by the smallest
   effective combination of external sources;
2. high-confidence identity enrichment, additions, corrections, and closures;
3. current menus;
4. useful photos linked to exact or strong menu items.

The finish line is a ranked implementation plan backed by real collectors and
measured samples—not perfect knowledge, unlimited sources, or paperwork.

## Success

Within six major cycles, deliver:

- a selected national backbone strategy and documented identity schema;
- measured national-quality and field-completeness results;
- at least one working backbone or enrichment collector worth integrating;
- measured national DoorDash and restaurant-website/ordering-page value;
- an Implement now / Review / Negotiate / Drop source ranking;
- exact next production builds, estimated lift, cost, risks, and rollback shape.

## Non-goals

Ghost-kitchen detection, opening dates, county-specific rollout systems, exact
cuisine or venue quotas, perfect demographic balance, customer acquisition
experiments, and Management-versus-Customer comparison counts are not required.
